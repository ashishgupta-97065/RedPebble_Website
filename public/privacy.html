Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information RedPebble.ai (“RedPebble”, “we”, “us”) collects when you visit redpebble.ai or get in touch with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

01Who we are

RedPebble.ai is an AI advisory practice. We are the controller of the personal information described here. For any privacy question or request, email ag@redpebble.ai.

02Information we collect

  • What you send us. When you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address, and, if you provide them, your phone number, company and message.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic request data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. We also count contact-form submissions in aggregate, without linking them to you.
  • No cookies or tracking. We do not set cookies, use advertising trackers or build profiles of visitors.

03How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and carry on any business conversation that follows.
  • To run, secure and troubleshoot the website.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how many people contact us.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

04Legal bases

Where laws such as the EU/UK GDPR apply, we rely on: steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract (answering your enquiry); our legitimate interests in running a secure website and responding to business contacts; and your consent where we specifically ask for it, which you can withdraw at any time.

05Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers contact-form messages to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and provides traffic analytics and security.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive and send.
  • YouTube (Google) hosts our video channel. See section 06 and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Content delivery networks (Google Fonts, unpkg, cdnjs and jsDelivr) serve the fonts and code libraries the site uses. Your browser connects to them directly, so they receive your IP address.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

06YouTube

RedPebble runs an official YouTube channel, RedPebble on YouTube, and uses YouTube API Services to manage and publish our videos and to show them on our website.

  • YouTube’s own terms apply. By watching or interacting with our YouTube content you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • What we access. Through YouTube API Services we access public channel and video information (such as titles, descriptions, thumbnails and view counts) and, only where you interact with our channel, the public comments and engagement associated with your YouTube account.
  • How we use it. To publish and manage our videos, show them on redpebble.ai, reply to comments and understand, in aggregate, how our content performs. We do not sell YouTube data or use it for advertising.
  • Storage and sharing. We do not set cookies of our own. Embedded or linked YouTube content may set Google cookies under Google’s policies. We do not share YouTube data with third parties except the service providers listed in section 05. Any YouTube API data we cache is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • Deletion and revoking access. To have us delete any YouTube data we hold about you, email ag@redpebble.ai; we will delete it within 7 days. You can also revoke our access to your data at any time through the Google security settings page.

07International transfers

Our providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect it.

08How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and related correspondence for as long as needed to deal with them and maintain our business relationship, and generally no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Hosting logs are kept according to Cloudflare’s standard retention periods.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it, or ask us to delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive a copy of it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent you have given; and
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email ag@redpebble.ai. We will respond within the time the law allows, usually within one month.

10Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, and we limit access to the information you send us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

11Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

12Links to other sites

Our site links to third-party sites such as LinkedIn and YouTube. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
